How do I export from Google Contacts?
  1. Open contacts.google.com and pick the label (group) you want, or select everyone.
  2. Choose Export, then Google CSV.
  3. Come back here and press Import Google Contacts.

🔒 Everything stays in this browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
